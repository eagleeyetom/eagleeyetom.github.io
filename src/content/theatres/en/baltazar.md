synopsis: |
  <p><strong>Lend me your eyes, Baltazars</strong> is a documentary by Dóra Elek that follows the Baltazár Theatre company on their journey to India. It shows the actors' encounters and experiences with naturalness, humour and poetic sensitivity.</p>
aboutShow: |
  <p>The actors of the Baltazár Theatre have felt a special bond with India for years. From the very beginning they practised yoga, and several of their performances are linked to Indian culture, especially “Happy Hour” from 2014, whose main theme is karma. Dóra Elek's film follows in their footsteps: to temples, markets, buses and the quiet of the sea, and back to the stage and the dressing room.</p>
  <p>The camera stays close to the people. The actors write in their diaries, pray, laugh, and ask about the future and about who they are, while the people they meet along the way respond with curiosity and warmth. The film runs 56 minutes and has English and Polish subtitles.</p>

about: |
  <p><strong>The Baltazár Theatre</strong> has been working in Budapest since 1998. It is a professional theatre company made up of actors with intellectual disabilities, which also runs educational and film projects. At the centre of its work are the actors' talent and the meeting that happens through art.</p>
history: |
  <p>The Baltazár Theatre has taken part in MASSKA before: in the 2025 edition it presented the performance “Dandelion and Dust”. This year it returns with a film that offers a look behind the scenes of its work and travels.</p>
