aboutShow: |
  <p>“LaVieEnRose / OtherDances” is a playful and intimate dance piece about the dancers' own personalities and thoughts, built around the idea of being different. Movement alternates with short statements from the performers: they talk about passing time, tiredness and limitations, about life in a virtual world, about the fear of losing the ability to move, about the people who are missed, and about the search for what makes us happy.</p>
  <p>On stage is the first inclusive dance company in Armenia: artists with and without disabilities perform side by side. According to the company, the performance is intended for audiences aged 16 and over.</p>
  <p>The statements in the performance are in Armenian. English subtitles are burned into the picture, and Polish subtitles can be switched on in the player.</p>
about: |
  <p>The <strong>Small Theatre</strong> is part of the Henrik Igityan National Centre for Aesthetics (NCA) in Yerevan, an institution that since 1978 has combined art education for children and young people (fine arts, theatre, music, dance) with exhibitions and theatre work. The Small Theatre's artistic director is Vahan Badalyan.</p>
  <p>The inclusive dance company was founded in 2014 as the first company of its kind in Armenia, in cooperation with the British Council and the UK's Candoco Dance Company. NCA is MASSKA's partner from Yerevan and develops artistic and educational activities, including work with people with disabilities. More about the organisation: <a href="https://nca.am" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">nca.am</a>.</p>
